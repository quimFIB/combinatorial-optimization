Threshold rounding, with t on a slider

Try: raise t just above ½ on each instance; switch to the triangle with a tail, whose LP mixes 0, ½ and 1. Static version: rounding a weighted 5-cycle.

What it shows. Weighted vertex cover: the LP value of each vertex (from the reference vertex_cover_lp, half-integral), and the vertices with $x_v \ge t$. At $t = \tfrac12$ the result is always a cover of weight at most $2 \cdot$ LP; above $\tfrac12$ edges with both ends at $\tfrac12$ lose their cover.

  • The deck's 5-cycle: all halves, LP 7, rounding pays 14, optimum 8.
  • A triangle with a tail: the triangle sits at ½, one tail vertex at 1, the other at 0.
  • An integral LP: every value is 0 or 1, rounding returns the optimum at any $t \le 1$.

Data: slides/viz/traces/u24.py; optima by brute force.

Primal–dual set cover, one raise at a time

Try: step through and watch each raise stop exactly when some set's bar is full. Static version: primal–dual set cover, traced.

What it shows. The deck's four elements on a cycle, each pair of neighbours forming a set ($S_0 = \{0,1\}$ cost 3, $S_1 = \{1,2\}$ cost 2, $S_2 = \{2,3\}$ cost 3, $S_3 = \{3,0\}$ cost 2). For each uncovered element in turn, its dual $y_e$ rises until a set containing it is fully paid; that set is bought. The bars show paid over cost.

Data: slides/viz/traces/u24.py runs the loop of the reference primal_dual_set_cover step by step and asserts the same $x = (1,1,0,1)$ and $y = (2,1,1,0)$.

Jain–Vazirani's clock, on a line

Try: drag the time slider from 0 to 6 and watch A open at 3.5 and B at 5. Static version: Jain–Vazirani on a line.

What it shows. Facilities A (at 0) and B (at 10), opening cost 4 each, clients at 1, 2, 9. Each client's $\alpha$ grows with time until it freezes; its bar shows how far it reaches. A facility's receipts are $\sum_j \max(0, \alpha_j - d_{ij})$, drawn as a bar that fills to its cost.

Data: the freeze times $\alpha = (\tfrac72, \tfrac72, 5)$ and opening times $\tfrac72$, 5 are from the reference jv_dual_ascent; the receipts at intermediate times are computed in the page from those. The kept facilities and the cost 12 are from jain_vazirani, and the brute-force costs from FacilityLocation.cost.
