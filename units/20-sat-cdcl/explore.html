CDCL, event by event

Try: step to the conflict, then the analysis: the learned clause and the first UIP light up. Then switch to the pigeonhole run, which ends unsatisfiable. Static versions: watched literals, the implication graph, the learned clause.

What it shows. A CDCL run as its implication graph (one column per decision level, filled nodes are decisions, arrows come from reason clauses) and its clause list (watched literals underlined, true in green, false struck through).

  • The deck's four clauses: decide x1, propagate x3, decide x2, propagate x4 and x5, conflict in (¬x4 ∨ ¬x5), learn (¬x2 ∨ ¬x3) with backjump to level 1, propagate ¬x2 from the learned clause, decide x4, propagate ¬x5: a model.
  • Three pigeons, two holes: one decision, a conflict, a learned unit clause at level 0, and a second conflict at level 0: unsatisfiable.

Data. slides/viz/traces/u20.py subclasses the reference Solver only to take snapshots; the first two decisions of the deck's run are scripted, the rest are the reference's own. Its satisfiability answer is checked against a plain reference run.
