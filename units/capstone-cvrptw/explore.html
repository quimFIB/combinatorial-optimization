A route on the clock, and where 4 fits

Try: step through the base route, the four insertion positions for customer 4, and the optimum. Static versions: the recurrence, constant-time insertion.

What it shows. The four-customer instance of the capstone deck, as a map and as a time axis: the green bars are time windows, the dark blocks are service, and the dashed ticks on the first view are latest starts. Start times come from the reference schedule and latest_starts, insertion costs from insertion_delta, recorded by slides/viz/traces/ucapstone.py. For an infeasible insertion schedule only returns None, so the times shown there continue the recurrence without checking deadlines, to point at the customer that runs late.

  • Base route: starts 30, 80, 120; latest starts 50, 100, 200; back at 170.
  • Inserting 4 first or between 1 and 2 makes customer 1 or 2 start after its window: infeasible.
  • Between 2 and 3: extra distance 60, customer 3 starts at 190 of a window closing at 200. That route is the optimum, distance 200.
  • At the end: feasible but 80 extra.
