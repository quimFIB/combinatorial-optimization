Gomory cuts, round by round

Try: step through the deck's example, then the four-round one. Static version: both cuts, drawn.

What it shows. The pure cutting-plane method on three two-variable integer programs. Each round solves the LP over the constraints so far, reads a Gomory fractional cut off the most fractional row of the optimal tableau, and adds it. The ring is the LP optimum the cut removes; the light red region is what the cuts have removed from the original LP region.

Recorded by slides/viz/traces/u08.py with the reference gomory_loop (exact fractions); each round's LP is re-solved with unit 02's reference two_phase on the constraints gomory_loop had at that point, and the LP bounds are checked against the ones gomory_loop returns.

Cover separation, live

Try: raise x4; lower x1 below 0.5; push everything to 1. Static versions: separating a cover, lifting.

What it shows. The deck's knapsack constraint $5x_0 + 5x_1 + 5x_2 + 5x_3 + 8x_4 \le 12$. For the LP values you set, every minimal cover is listed with its cost $\sum_{j \in C}(1 - x_j^*)$; a cost below 1 means the cover inequality is violated. The most violated cover is then lifted, in index order, and its left side evaluated at $x^*$. Covers, costs and lifting are enumerated in the page from the constraint written in this file (it is small enough).

  • At the start, $x^* = (1, 0.7, 0.7, 0, 0)$: cover $\{0,1,2\}$ costs 0.6, the deck's example, and its lifted form $x_0 + x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + 2x_4 \le 2$ is still violated (2.4).
  • Raise $x_4$: covers containing item 4 get cheap, and the order changes.
